description: Turn a process you only know how to do yourself into an SOP a VA can follow
---

Turn one process out of the operator's head into a written SOP their assistant could follow.

Start by asking which process hurts most. Offer examples so they do not have to think: turnover between guests, onboarding a new landlord, handling a maintenance call at 9 PM, taking an inbound lead, move-in day.

Then tell them to describe it out loud, badly, in whatever order it comes. Dictating from a phone is ideal. Rambling is fine and they should not try to organize it.

Follow the `mtr-sop-writer` skill.

The important part is the interrogation: **ask about every step that is ambiguous before you write the final version.** Those ambiguities are the exact places a VA would guess wrong, and finding them is most of the value here. Do not skip it to produce a tidy document faster.

Write the result to `sop-<process>.md` and tell them to run the process against it once themselves before handing it to anyone.
