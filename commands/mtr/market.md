Research demand for 30 to 90 day furnished housing in one market, then score it.

Ask which city — plus state or region, wherever they are — unless `mtr-profile.md` or `CLAUDE.md` already names one and they confirm it.

Follow the `mtr-market-scout` skill. Produce the demand-driver table across all five categories, with a verification source named for every row.

Then score the market out of 10 using the scorecard in that skill, state the go / no-go, and say which single row you would verify first if they only had twenty minutes.

**Label confidence honestly on every row.** You are producing a scout report they still have to check, not a decision. If you are not sure something is current, write that instead of guessing. An operator who signs a lease on a stale data point loses real money.

Write the result to `market-<city>.md`.
